The phenomenon of **zitterbewegung**, which means "bouncing back and forth" in German, was originally proposed by [[@^Schrodinger30]], as a very high-frequency vibration at twice the Compton frequency (see [[planck relation]]) ([[@Hestenes90]]). It is largely beyond current experimental techniques to measure (e.g., frequency of around $1.6 x 10^{21}$ Hz), but some indications have been observed ([[@GerritsmaKirchmairZahringerEtAl10]]; [[@QuHamnerGongEtAl13]]). The de Broglie version of the [[pilot wave]] framework predicts such a phenomenon ([[@DaganBush20]]).
