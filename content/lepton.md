A **lepton** is one of two categories of [[fermion]] particles, which are those having [[spin]] 1/2 and a non-zero rest mass. The [[electron]] is the prototypical member of the lepton category, which also includes uncharged [[neutrino]]s, and 2 other [[generation]]s of electrons (_muon_ and _tau_). The [[weak#electroweak]] framework provides the comprehensive model of the leptons in the [[Standard Model]].

The charged leptons have an electrical charge of -1, and each have an [[antimatter]] particle with the opposite charge.

{id="table_leptons" title="charged leptons"}
| Name     | Mass kg           | Mass MeV/$c^2$ | Mass ratio | Antiparticle |
|----------|-------------------|----------------|------------|--------------|
| electron | $9.1 \times 10^{-31}$ | 0.51           | 1          | positron     |
| muon     | $1.88 \times 10^{-28}$ | 105.7          | 207        | antimuon     |
| tau      | $3.17 \times 10^{-27}$ | 1,776          | 3,477      | antitau      |

The higher generations are unstable and rapidly decay back into an electron along with various neutrinos.

There are also 3 corresponding generations of neutrinos, which have much smaller mass differences. The antineutrinos differ only in their spin direction.
