The **back-reaction** is the process by which a [[particle]] generates a corresponding wave, as in the case of a charged [[electron]] creating the corresponding [[Maxwell]] field. This is a thorny and unsolved problem in the [[Standard Model]], where [[particle]]s are infinitesimal points, and thus would generate infinitely strong fields in their immediate vicinity ([[@Rohrlich00]]; [[@Rohrlich99]]; [[@Rohrlich97]]; [[@FordOConnell91]]; [[@FordOConnell93]]; [[@Baez20]]).
