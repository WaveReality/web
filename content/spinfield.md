The **spinfield** model is based on a field of [[harmonic oscillator]] values using [[harmonic oscillator#complex numbers]] to represent the [[spin]] and other associated properties of [[stochastic particles]] that drive and couple with a [[quantum wave]] field.

The core problem solved by this model is to make discrete, stochastic particle motion sufficiently smooth that the resulting coupled wave field can effectively communicate the impacts of other particles without being swamped by high-frequency oscillations emerging from the driving of the wave by the particle itself.

This smoothing is accomplished by the discrete particle broadcasting its own parameters into the spin field, so that the entire neighborhood of cubes on the lattice is already prepared in advance of wherever the particle might move. 

Wavelength = energy, which is what is broadcast. current energy increases smoothly.
