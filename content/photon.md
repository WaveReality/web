[[PalmerducaQin25]] show that it is impossible to derive the spin angular momentum, separate from orbital angular momentum, from massless bosons such as the photon and the graviton. The assertion that the photon has spin 1 derives from the two polarization directions of light. It is nothing like the quantum spin associated with massive particles, and is instead really just a relabeling of properties already known in Maxwell's equations.
