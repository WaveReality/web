The **Standard Model** of quantum physics defines a set of fundamental [[particle]]s and their properties, which interact according to force fields carried by [[bosons]]. It has been tested empirically in high-energy collider and cosmic-ray experiments, and through its astrophysical implications, and has been found to be consistent with all of the data accumulated to date.

This serves as the target of explanation for this project. All of the pages describing elements of this standard model, including more basic physics principles, are collected under this heading.
