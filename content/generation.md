The **generation** property of [[particle]]s refers to the 3 different **rest mass** variants of each type of [[fermion]], forming a **mass heirarchy**. For example:

* The [[electron]] is a [[lepton]] with charge -1, rest mass $9.109 x 10^{-31}$ kg.
* The **muon** is just like an electron, with mass ~207x heavier. It is unstable and decays into an electron.
* The **tau** is just like a muon except it is 17x heavier, and is also unstable and ultimately ends up as an electron as well, through multiple different pathways.

Lepton generation is a weakly conserved property. It is conserved for example when a muon decays into an electron, because it also must release one muon neutrino and one electron antineutrino. However, [[neutrino]]s themselves do not conserve their generation properties, due to **neutrino oscillation** among the three different generations. This strange phenomenon may provide important insight into the nature of [[spin]] and its relationship to mass, all tied up in the [[weak]] interaction.
