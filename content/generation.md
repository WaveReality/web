The **generation** property of [[particle]]s refers to the 3 different **rest mass** variants of each type of [[fermion]], for example:

* The [[electron]] is a [[lepton]] with charge -1, rest mass $9.109 x 10^{-31}$ kg.
* The **muon** is just like an electron, with mass ~207x heavier. It is unstable and decays into an electron.
* The **tau** is just like a muon except it is 17x heavier.

Lepton generation is a weakly conserved property, for example when a muon decays into an electron, it also releases one muon neutrino and one electron antineutrino.
