A **quark** is the other type of [[fermion]] [[particle]], beyond the [[lepton]]s (e.g., the [[electron]]). Different flavors of quarks combine in various ways to create the **proton** and **neutron** constituents of the atomic nucleus.

The intimate connection between quarks and leptons can be seen in the decay of a **pion** (composed of quarks) into electrons and [[neutrino]]s, with no trace of "quarkiness" remaining.

Thus, our initial goal is to understand the core properties of all such particles by focusing on the simpler case of the leptons, and only after that is all working will we turn our focus on quarks and the strong force that uniquely binds them together.
