**Renormalization** refers to a family of techniques that are needed to manage the infinite sums that occur in [[field theory]] frameworks such as [[QED]]. These infinite sums result from the need to include all possible paths in the path-integral formulation, where [[virtual particles]] can be created and destroyed at any point. In the absence of some kind of **minimum length / energy scale** or **cutoff**, infinitely many such paths exist. As the length scale gets smaller, the energy scale gets higher, according to the fundamental [[Planck relation]], and there is also the possibility that physics beyond that described by the [[Standard Model]] could exist at these higher energy scales (i.e., unknown unknowns).

The essential character of the renormalization approach is to recognize that the empirically-determined mass and charge values for particles (e.g., the [[electron]]) must include _all_ of the relevant high-energy contributions, which are evidently finite as these particles have finite empirically-determined rest masses. Therefore, one can essentially _start_ from these known parameters (the fully **dressed** values), and then work backward to compute the **bare** values that show up in the Standard Model [[Lagrangian]] equations.

An agnostic interpretation of renormalization is provided by **effective field theory** ([[@Weinberg79]]; [[@Pich98]]; [[@Weinberg16]]), which holds that the current theory is only known to be accurate up to an effective energy level, and the renormalization process is a way of performing the calculations up to that effective level, while accepting that we don't necessarily yet have the final, full "grand unified theory" (GUT) of everything.

A more radical approach would be to conclude from the manifest success of the current Standard Model, and the apparent "desert" of higher-energy particles that can be inferred from many existing sources of data (see [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desert_(particle_physics)) for more discussion), that in fact the current model is all there is to it. In this case, the effective _ultraviolet cutoff_ implied by renormalization represents an actual positive assertion about the nature of the physics, which would be consistent with a discretized space as in the [[cellular automaton]] (CA) framework.
