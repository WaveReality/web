A **fermion** is a massive [[particle]] with [[spin]] 1/2, which is described by the [[Dirac]] or [[Weyl]] wave equation in the [[Standard Model]].

There are two types of fermions: [[lepton]]s and [[quark]]s. We are primarily focused on leptons in the current scope here. The [[weak#electroweak]] framework provides the comprehensive model of the leptons, including [[neutrinos]], [[electrons]], and the different [[generation]]s of each of these. They get their mass through interactions with the [[Higgs]] field in this framework.
